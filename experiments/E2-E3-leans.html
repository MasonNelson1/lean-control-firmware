Seat sensors during leans: bolts as before vs loosened

Mason in the chair, 2026-11-21. Raw sensor counts, 1-second averages. 372 = zero force. Left and right sensors aren't visible until the new firmware is uploaded.

Takeaways
  • Loosening the bolts didn't change the lean response. The two runs track each other.
  • The front sensor sits at zero force except during forward, so back leans come from the back sensor alone.
  • Active neutral reads 100–150 counts higher on the back sensor than passive neutral, about half of a back-touch lean.
  • Back-touch loads the back sensor more than back-pivot, because the backrest takes the load.
