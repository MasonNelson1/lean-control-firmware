Plus vs rectangle: the full comparison

Mason's sweep on each layout: plus F5 B5 L5 R5 (E5, 11/21) and rectangle (E9, 11/22). Same math (centre of pressure in plate inches, from measured hole positions, empty plate subtracted) and the same method for both.

Plus (E5)Rectangle (E9)
  • Back lean doubled. The plate now registers 1.96" of back lean, against 0.88" on the plus, and steps going back rose from 5.5 to 11.9. On the plus, a back lean mostly unloaded the side sensors instead of loading the back one.
  • Forward and left/right are about the same. Those were already fine on the plus with the all-sensor math.
  • Sensor-limited resolution rose 2–7× (back 26 → 184). Every sensor now carries load at neutral; the plus front sensor carried 1%.
  • One cost: the rectangle sees the slump more clearly (0.74" between passive and active neutral, against 0.40"). Two-posture calibration handles it.
  • Correction: earlier today I said resolution was the same as the plus. That compared numbers measured two different ways. Measured the same way, back is twice as good.
