## What to build

2. **The new layout: centre-of-pressure (CoP) math in plate inches.**
   - CoP x = Σ(fᵢ·xᵢ)/Σfᵢ, CoP y = Σ(fᵢ·yᵢ)/Σfᵢ, where fᵢ = (rawᵢ − empty-plate baseline) × gainᵢ.
   - Sensor positions and per-sensor gains (default 1.0) are config values, not magic numbers.
   - The baseline is the empty-plate reading. The firmware already tracks floors and calibration; reuse that where sensible.
3. **Per-rider calibration comes first; gains are a second-order correction.**
   - The rider calibrates in *active neutral* (sitting upright and engaged), which sets the CoP origin.
   - Full-lean distance is set per direction (forward, back, left, right) relative to that neutral, plus a deadzone.
   - Map the CoP offset to the same speed/turn outputs the existing math produces, so the rest of the pipeline (mode state machine, RPM PID, clamps, seat gate, rail disarm) is untouched.

## Design and why
- **Runtime layout switch.** There's a new "Sensor layout" setting (PLUS or CORNER, default CORNER) in the config registry, saved to EEPROM.
  - The layout is recorded when the rider calibrates. If the live setting doesn't match it, LEAN stays stopped until they recalibrate. Entering LEAN already forces a recalibration, so this is a backstop.
- **One function per layout, not a strategy class.** All the math is in plain functions in [lean_layout.h](<worktree>/wheelchair-firmware/src/driveModes/lean_layout.h), with no hardware or settings inside, so tests call them directly.
  - The plus math moved there unchanged.
  - Both layouts go through the same response curves and reverse-turn flip (your comment is kept word for word). Turn mixing and everything after it (mode state machine, RPM PID, clamps, seat gate, rail disarm) is untouched.
- **Corner math:** force = (raw − empty plate) × gain, floored at zero. The centre of pressure (CoP) is computed from that in plate inches, and its offset from the rider's active neutral drives each axis.
  - Each axis has a deadzone in inches (default 0.25"). Output ramps up from the deadzone edge with no jump, to full at each direction's full-lean distance.
  - The plus layout's normalised deadband doesn't apply on corner.
- **Per-rider calibration:**
  - A short press of the calibrate button sets the neutral CoP. It uses the same averaged readings the plus layout already takes.
  - Holding the button and leaning all four ways now sets that rider's full-lean distance per direction. This is the corner version of the plus layout's dynamic gains, and like them it rejects the attempt if one direction is weak.
  - The configured defaults (+2.0 / −1.9 / −1.7 / +1.6") apply when no hold calibration has been done.
- **Sensor positions are compile-time constants, not settings.** Each saved profile has 415 bytes. With the existing settings at 299 bytes, the 13 corner tuning settings (empty-plate baselines, gains, deadzone, reach) bring it to 400 bytes, so eight more floats wouldn't fit. Positions only change when someone re-drills the plate, and a reflash goes with that.
- **Seat detection is unchanged:** still the sum of the four channels against the rider weight estimate. A test checks it gives the same seated/empty answer on both layouts.
- **Proof the plus layout is unchanged:** I wrote `plus_golden_test` before touching any code. It hashes every output across 9,310 samples, using values generated from 8712aef. A deliberate change to the reverse-turn threshold makes it fail.

## Design choices
- **Relaxed (passive) posture: a second press within 10 s.** The first press, sitting upright, sets neutral, and the calibrate LED then blinks slowly. A second press while relaxed records the relaxed posture instead of retaking neutral.
  - I rejected a dashboard button: the rider won't have a laptop mid-game, and it would need a protocol change.
  - I rejected locking the chair for 10 s after each press, because that breaks "no second press = behaves as now".
  - With a relaxed posture, speed stays at zero from upright to relaxed plus 0.15" (new setting 96), and forward reach is measured from the relaxed point. The posture is kept in RAM only.
  - A "relaxed" press more than 1.5" from neutral is treated as a lean and rejected.
  - In the replay, slumping now gives 0 speed instead of +0.14.
- **Automatic empty-seat zero, in RAM only.** It needs DISARM, an empty seat, and every channel steady within 12 counts for 3 s. It won't move a baseline more than 30 counts from the configured value, so a bag on the seat doesn't get zeroed out. Your reseated readings (383/396/420/404) are adopted, and a dashboard edit of a baseline wins over it.
  - The thresholds come from E9 empty-plate noise (σ about 1–2 counts) and are compile-time constants, so they cost no storage. Profile storage is now 7 bytes from full on the host build (15 on the Teensy).
- **Dashboard:** the four tiles move to the corners they measure (for example "Back-left (F)"), and a plate diagram shows the live CoP with a 2 s trail, neutral (+), relaxed posture (○), the no-output box and the full-lean box. The plus view, and firmware that doesn't report a layout, keep the old display.
  - The firmware sends a new 1 Hz `DBG CORNER` line with the box edges already computed, so the dashboard doesn't duplicate the math.
  - The new settings show up automatically through the existing schema-driven settings UI.
  - I checked it headless on the E9 replay at desktop and phone width and fixed one overlap of the corner tiles.
- **Mid-ride dead-channel check:** output stops and LEAN disarms if the CoP moves more than 0.75" between control ticks. From E9:
  - Healthy riding peaked at 0.54" per 50 ms (11.7 in/s), and getting out of the seat at 1.17" per 50 ms. At the slowest control period that's at most 0.36" per tick.
  - A dropped channel moves the CoP at once: F by at least 1.96", B at least 1.07", R at least 0.53" (1.06" in 95% of postures), L at least 0.22".
  - I also tried a diagonal-balance check (front-left + back-right against front-right + back-left), but healthy and dead readings overlap in E9, so I left it out.
- **Review item 5, done differently.** A short press does not reset the hold-calibrated reach. A hold calibration always ends in DISARM, and every LEAN entry needs a short press, so resetting on a short press would mean the hold reach could never be used. Instead it's dropped when a reach setting changes, when the seat has been empty for 3 s (a new rider), or when the profile changes.

## Behaviour changes
- **Plus-layout steering is still bit-identical to 8712aef.** The golden test no longer hashes the rail flag, because:
- **The rail-fault threshold moved from 100 to 340 counts on both layouts, as intended.** A reading between 101 and 340 used to pass and now disarms LEAN.
- **Selecting a profile** now resets every field to its default before applying it, on both layouts. An old profile now gets defaults instead of inheriting the previous profile's values.
- **Corner layout only:**
  - Neutral calibration is rejected if any corner carries under 50 counts or under 5% of the load. LEAN stays stopped and the LED stays on.
  - The 0.75" CoP-jump disarm.
  - Minimum reach is now 1.2" (was 0.6").
  - The relaxed-posture press and the empty-seat zero.
- **Bluetooth transmit buffer is now 1024 bytes** (was 512). The worst 1 Hz burst is about 700 bytes, with room for your `DBG VESCPROBE`/`DBG VESCFW` lines. Expect a small merge in `main.cpp`'s 1 Hz block.
- **`DBG SENSORS`** now carries only `layout=`; the CoP, neutral and reach fields moved to `DBG CORNER`. Over USB/replay the dashboard console now hides `DBG` lines too, as Bluetooth already did.

## Open questions
1. **Collisions could trip the 0.75" jump disarm.** A hard chair-to-chair hit may move the CoP fast enough. It needs a bump test on the chair; `SENSOR_RAIL_DISARM` turns this check off along with the rail one.
2. **A channel dying while barely loaded isn't caught.** At back-touch, a dead L moves the CoP only 0.27" (R 0.62"); a test pins this down. The unplug-on-the-bench test will show what a pulled wire actually reads.
3. **With a relaxed posture, the default 2.0" forward reach is too long:** forward reaches only 0.50 speed. A hold calibration gives Mason 1.32" and full speed. Decide whether to change the default or require the hold.
4. **Collision risk with the plus-layout dynamic gains:** they still only reload on reboot or profile change. That predates this branch.
5. The relaxed posture only affects forward/back. Turning keeps the upright x and its 0.25" deadzone.
