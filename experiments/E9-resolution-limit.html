What limits resolution: the sensors or the rider?

Rectangle layout, Mason's full lean range (E9). Steps = lean range ÷ 2σ. Sensor noise is measured on the empty seat (±1–1.4 counts per channel); rider sway is measured while Mason held each lean.

  • The sensors and placement resolve 100–180 steps. On their own, they place the centre of pressure to ±0.006–0.008".
  • The rider is the limit. Even holding still, Mason's weight drifts ±0.05" side to side and ±0.08" front to back. That's real body motion, and any sensor would see it.
  • Smoothing barely helps: averaging over 0.5 s only trims the sway 10–30%, because it's slow drift, not jitter.
