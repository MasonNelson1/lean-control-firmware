Which sensors move when you lean

Mason, E4 (22:18), F5 B2 L5 R5. Change in raw counts from active neutral to each held lean. Bars right of zero = more load; left = less load.

What it shows
  • Forward: the front rises +222, but left and right each rise about as much (+171, +164), and the back drops −145. Two-thirds of the forward signal is not on the front sensor.
  • Back: the back rises +138, but the right side drops even more (−162), and the left drops −82. Side sensors unloading is as big a back-lean signal as the back sensor loading.
  • Left/right: the side sensors swing 400–500 counts. That's the strongest signal on the chair.
  • So reading the front sensor alone for forward and the back sensor alone for back throws most of the information away. The centre-of-pressure math uses all four.
