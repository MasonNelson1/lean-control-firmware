## 1. Findings, most severe first

**F1. Arming LEAN or REMOTE while rolling brakes at 15 A in one step.**
*Confirmed by simulation; real defect.* [DriveController.cpp:225-243](<worktree>/wheelchair-firmware/src/DriveController.cpp:225), reached from [DriveController.cpp:493-496](<worktree>/wheelchair-firmware/src/DriveController.cpp:493) and [:521](<worktree>/wheelchair-firmware/src/DriveController.cpp:521).
- **Scenario:** the rider is seated in DISARM and pushing at 60 RPM (1.9 m/s), then presses LEAN.
- **What happens:** LEAN always needs a fresh calibration, so `command.stopped` leads to `stopOrRelease`. `driving_` is false and no stop is running, so `parked()` holds the chair. The first tick sends `Brake 15.0` to both wheels. No ramp-up and no `StopController` are involved.
- **What the rider feels:** a full-force grab of up to 0.3 g. In LEAN it lasts until the chair stops, because calibration needs a still second.
- **REMOTE:** same thing, as a 10–20 ms jab until the first centred packet arrives.
- **Why it matters:** `STOP_ONSET_S` exists precisely because "0.38 g at once pitches the rider forward", and the calibration button deliberately ignores presses at speed. Arming doesn't, and no test covers it.
- **Smallest fix:** refuse to arm LEAN or REMOTE above `CALIBRATE_MAX_RPM`, and log "slow down first". Alternatively, in `applySeat`, start `stop_.begin(measured)` when entering a held state with the wheels at or above `STOP_DONE_RPM`, so the onset ramp applies. Add a ride test either way.

**F2. Turning still overshoots by 20–31° from a full-rate turn.**
*Confirmed in the host sim; tuning defect in this branch's own constant.* [drive_constants.h:128-129](<worktree>/wheelchair-firmware/src/drive_constants.h:128)

I held a full sideways lean to 160°/s, released it, and measured the chair's turn after release:

| VESC min speed | Lean | Turn rate | Turn after release | Still turning for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| default 15.5 RPM | full | 160°/s | 31° | 0.7 s |
| lowered to 3 RPM | full | 160°/s | 21° | 0.57 s |
| lowered to 3 RPM | half | 51°/s | 7° | — |

- The 900°/s² turn deceleration alone accounts for 14° (160² / 1800). The 25 ms sensor filter and the wheels' lag add the rest.
- On the default VESC setting, a half lean pivots not at all: any pivot under about 94°/s sits inside the VESC's minimum-speed zone. That is "nothing, nothing, then everything" for turning too. It's flagged, but it should block riding until `s_pid_min_erpm` is lowered.
- **Fix:** raise `TURN_DECEL_DEG_S2` (1800 gives about 7° of command coast) or make it a dashboard setting so the team can tune it wheels-off. Add a test asserting the overshoot after release.

**F3. EEPROM saves stall the control loop with no check that the chair is at rest.**
*Plausible; the timing is unverified.* [main.cpp:332](<worktree>/wheelchair-firmware/src/main.cpp:332)
- Writes are allowed whenever the chair is in `DISARM && !stopping`, which includes a seated rider pushing by hand.
- A dashboard Save or a profile write can block `loop()` for hundreds of ms. The code itself expects long stalls: see the watchdog feed and the `dt` cap.
- During the stall no CAN commands go out. With the recommended VESC timeout of 200 ms and timeout brake current of 15 A, a chair being pushed by hand gets a sudden brake.
- **Fix:** also require both VESCs fresh and both wheels under `STOP_DONE_RPM` before any Set, Save, Revert, Defaults or Profile write.

**F4. The stop's fallback can brake *less* on a steep downhill.**
*Plausible; real logic gap.* [StopController.h:278-288](<worktree>/wheelchair-firmware/src/motion/StopController.h:278)
- "Wheels not slowing" is read as "Allow Braking is off", and the stop switches to a 15 A brake.
- On a slope where the speed loop is already braking at its current limit, that switch weakens the braking.
- **Fix:** `VescStatus.current` is already decoded. Only declare the ramp ineffective when the measured motor current is near zero.

**F5. Braking out of reverse flips the turn direction at once.**
*Confirmed by reading; minor.* [LeanDriveMode.cpp:304-319](<worktree>/wheelchair-firmware/src/driveModes/LeanDriveMode.cpp:304)
- `forwardTravel` is clamped at 0 for negative travel, so a forward lean while still rolling backward un-flips the reverse turn rule immediately.
- This is the mirror of the forward-speed case this branch fixed. It's low-severity because reverse is limited to half speed.

**Matters of taste**
- [drive_constants.h:29](<worktree>/wheelchair-firmware/src/drive_constants.h:29): the comment says "x% of 11 mph for testing", but the value is exactly 11 mph, so knob position 4 is 4.9 m/s. For first supervised rides I'd cap the knob in software, for example at position 2.
- Spec B "safe state within 0.10 s": `VESC_STALE_MS = 100` plus one 5 ms tick is about 105 ms just to detect the fault. Call it at the limit, not met.
- "Speed-up ends at once" (`acceleration_ = 0` in `rpm_setpoint_pid.h`) is a deliberate jerk step. It's defensible, but worth feeling on the bench.

## 2. Checked and found sound
- **Faults:** sensor at an ADC rail and silent VESC disarm the chair and then hold it, including with a rider aboard until DISARM is pressed. Recovery after a watchdog restart and blind braking at boot work as described. Loss of a sensor can't create motion, because only gained load counts.
- **Modes:** the state machine handles accidental presses, DISARM always wins, DISARM held after a stop never turns into Assist, Assist is re-armed only after a 1 s delay, and the profile hold needs a seated rider in DISARM. A rider sitting back down never resumes driving without a new calibration.
- **Every stop path:** DISARM press, empty seat, lost remote, soft stop, mode switch at speed, and calibration requested while creeping.
- **Assist:** it can't start or keep the chair moving by itself.
- **Remote:** recentring after timeouts and soft stops; safe across `millis()` rollover.
- **Units:** about 31.6 RPM per m/s, 58.2 ERPM per wheel RPM, and the stop timings in the comments match my arithmetic.
- **VESC behaviour:** the CAN encodings and min-ERPM behaviour match `foc_math.c`, `mcpwm_foc.c` and `comm_can.c`. Brake-current mode can't reverse a wheel.
- **Settings:** EEPROM layout fits (4153 of 4284 bytes); commit-marker writes with read-back verification; profile validate-and-rollback; config writes gated to DISARM.
- **Dashboard:** the HEALTH and LEAN_RANGES lines match its parsers.
