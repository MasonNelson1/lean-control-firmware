Do we meet Spec P today?

Spec P: stop from top speed (4.92 m/s) within 3.0 s. Charles thought we're close. This estimate says today's settings miss it by about a second at top speed, because the battery ceiling makes braking so weak above 3 m/s. The stop at 17:08 agrees: from 4.3 m/s, the chair was still at 3 m/s after 1.5 s of braking. estimate

Fix 2 is what gets us under 3.0 s. Fix 1 alone makes the top-speed stop slightly slower, because it caps braking at the slow end too. With both, the 20 A setting becomes the limit for most of the stop, which is why 22–25 A is worth testing after the braking profile is in.

Test order

  1. Ride the −20 A setting. Hard stops from speed, including turning while stopping. Pass = no ABS_OVER_CURRENT in the log.
  2. Measure the top-speed stop time, to check this estimate against Spec P.
  3. Raise the regen limit one step at a time (−5, then −7.5 A). Use a mid-charge pack and a teammate rider, with a spotter and logging on.
  4. Bench-test the braking-profile firmware with the wheels off, then on the court.

Change one thing per test. If two changes go in together and something faults, you can't tell which one caused it.
