VESC settings, explained

Read over CAN from both controllers on 2026-11-22 (firmware 6.06, VESC 6 MkVI). "Factory" is the VESC firmware default. Rows marked in the left margin are the changes I recommend.

  • Most of it is factory or set by the motor wizard, and that's fine. 42 of 319 settings differ from factory, mostly motor measurements. 24 differ between left and right.
  • Changes I'd make: timeout 250 ms with ~10 A brake (stop if the Teensy dies), minimum speed 900 → ~300 (lets the chair creep), match the current limits on both sides, turn on battery voltage reporting.
  • Leave alone: regen −3 A (deliberate, battery charge limit), low-battery taper, everything the motor wizard set.
  • Nothing has been written. Changes go in one approved batch, with the original settings saved to restore.

Battery voltage: why 58.2 V vs 57 V matters, and why you haven't seen it

A LiFePO4 pack sits at about 3.3 V per cell almost all the time, which is the ≈52 V Mason sees. Only right off the charger is it at 3.64 V per cell (58.2 V), and it falls below 57 V within minutes of use. So the risk is narrow: regen braking on a freshly charged pack can push it over 57 V, and the VESC cuts out mid-brake. That would explain never seeing it. Battery-voltage reporting (STATUS_5) lets us watch it instead of guessing. Mason is right that the VESC 6 is a "60 V" controller; its firmware trips at 57 V to leave margin.
