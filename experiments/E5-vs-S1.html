Back sensor at B2 vs B5: same rider, same sweep

Mason, 2026-11-21. S1 = back sensor at B2 (22:40). E5 = back sensor at B5 (22:55). Everything else is the same (F5 L5 R5, no plate bolts).

What it shows
  • B5 is better for backward lean: about 40% more range, and the flat middle section is gone in both back leans.
  • Steps of control going back rose from ≈6 to ≈10. Forward rose from ≈10 to ≈18. Left/right rose a little.
  • Caveat: one sweep each, and Mason leaned a bit faster at B5. Some of the gain may be lean depth, but the back curve's shape changing is the cleaner signal.
