## The speed clamp and the 17:08 overcurrent
The premise is off: the council did not replace `MAX_RPM_COMMAND_DIFF`. It still clamps to measured ± 50 RPM, in normal driving (turn gets the allowance first) and in the stop ramp ([DriveController.cpp:240](<worktree>/wheelchair-firmware/src/DriveController.cpp:240)). It adds `limitLead` and `unwind` on top.

What a stop does now:
- **Ramp:** from the lower of sent and measured speed, at 3.7 m/s² (117 RPM/s), with a 0.1 s soft start, down to 18 RPM (the VESC minimum-speed setting of 15.5 plus 2.5).
- **Below 18 RPM:** 15 A brake current on both wheels until under 3 RPM for 150 ms.
- **After that:** the chair is held; a moving wheel gets brake current of 1 A per RPM, between 3 and 15 A.
- **Normal driving below 18 RPM:** a wheel faster than its command gets that same proportional brake current instead of a speed command.

Does it avoid the fault? **Partly.** Below 18 RPM the VESC is told a fixed 15 A, not asked to chase a speed, and noise in the speed reading stops mattering there. Above 18 RPM, a regen-limited wheel that can't keep up with the ramp still gets a command at measured − 50, so the VESC still demands full braking, exactly as today. At kp 0.004, about 4 RPM of error already saturates it. The −20 A VESC limit is the real cap, and see item 1 above.

I left the VESC minimum speed at 15.5, though the chair's VESCs are at 300 ERPM (≈5.2 RPM). That keeps the fixed 15 A brake handling the stop from 18 RPM down, instead of from about 7.7 RPM.
