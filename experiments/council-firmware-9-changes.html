1Stopping and faults

Each chart: chair speed over 4 seconds. The dashed line is the moment something happens.

Stop leaning in LEAN: still no coasting, before or after

Sitting up has always meant the motors slow the chair. The old code did it too.

Before: the last walking-pace bit was up to the motor controller. Depending on its firmware, it let go or braked. Parked at neutral on a slope, the chair rolled.

After: the last bit uses brake current, then the chair is held.

Wheelchair basketball coasts on purpose

On this branch, coasting happens in only two places: Assist (never brakes) and DISARM with someone in the seat (and only once the stop is over). Pressing DISARM at speed now brakes at about 0.38 g instead of letting go. If "sit up and glide" is wanted, it would be a new feature. See the open questions.

What a stop looks like now speed-knob 2, illustrative

How long until the speed command ends from code comments

The design spec (CDR Spec B) asks for under 0.5 s. Only knob 1 makes it. Going faster would take about 8.7 m/s², more than the motors can deliver.

2Steering

Full-lean turn rate at a standstill from code defaults

Before, turning was a share of the speed knob, so knob 4 turned four times faster than knob 1. Now it is 160°/s on every knob. Assumes a 0.60 m track width, which is an estimate in the code; measure it on the chair.

Biggest turn allowed vs. speed from code defaults

Now capped by a 2.5 m/s² sideways-acceleration limit. Before, a full lean at speed asked for a spin (one wheel backwards). These are the speeds commanded, before smoothing.

Steering while speeding up illustrative

Before, both wheels hit the same acceleration limit, so the turn vanished until the speed-up ended. Now speed and turn are smoothed separately. Releasing a turn stops it 3× faster than it built up (1800 vs 600 °/s²).

Reverse illustrative

A back lean that starts while rolling forward only brakes to a stop. To reverse, come back to neutral, then lean back again. Reverse tops out at half the knob speed (also in REMOTE).

Turn direction in reverse from code

The team's −0.3 / −0.9 rule is kept, but it now fades instead of jumping, and it is off while the chair still rolls forward. Before, leaning back and right at speed steered left.

Remote (REMOTE mode)

stick held over→signal gap > 0.5 s→ignored until the stick is centred
stop button→works in LEAN and Assist too
full stick→same turn and reverse limits as LEAN

Before, a held stick drove off again the moment the signal came back, and the stop button only worked in REMOTE.

3Calibration

Neutral LEAN, short press

press→let go→0.3 s settle→sit still 1 s (averaged)→ready

Before: a 40 ms snapshot taken while your hand was on the button, which froze everything else on the chair while it ran. Now it waits for you to sit still, and gives up after 6 s if you don't.

Range LEAN, hold 1 s → fast blink

let go→neutral→lean F, B, L, R fully→press again→4 ranges saved

90% of your reach = full speed. A direction leaned less than 120 counts keeps its old range. The chair ends in DISARM.

Tare DISARM, seat empty, hold 2 s

empty seat→hold 2 s→1 s still→4 sensor zeros saved

Refused (or cancelled) if the seat reads occupied. Redo it after moving the cushion or the sensors.

The lean math

Only load a sensor gains counts. These sensors use two wires, so a broken wire reads the same as "no load". Before, a broken front wire looked like a back lean. Now it just turns that direction off.

Am I seated? illustrative

Now: seated above 200 counts after 0.3 s, and empty only below 120 counts for 0.08 s. Before: one threshold with no delay, tied to the last rider's calibration, so a hard lean could flicker it to "empty".

Also new: while you sit still at neutral for 2 s or more, neutral slowly follows sensor drift (time constant 10 s, never more than 15% of the range). This stops the chair creeping after a long sit.

5Assist, testing, open questions

Assist: one push, one boost illustrative

Before, the boost grew with speed, and speed grew with the boost, so a nudge could run the chair up to the limit by itself. Now the motor only helps after a real push: a short speed-up of at least 1 m/s² that ends within 1 s. The help fades out over 0.7 s. A long downhill roll never earns a boost.

Assist rules

DISARM for 1 s already→hold DISARM 0.5 s→Assist
DISARM held from LEAN→stays a stop
seat empties→Assist ends; pick it again
near the speed limit→boost fades to 0
sensor or VESC fault→boost dropped, no brake
remote stop button→brakes and holds

Test wheels-off first

  1. VESC Tool, both controllers: Speed PID "Allow Braking" ON. Ramp ERPM/s at 25000 or more. CAN status at 50 Hz.
  2. VESC Tool: App Timeout 200 ms, Timeout Brake Current 15 A. These decide what a frozen Teensy does.
  3. Write down s_pid_min_erpm and the VESC firmware version. Set the dashboard's "VESC minimum speed" to min ERPM ÷ 58.2.
  4. Lean right: the left wheel must spin faster. If not, the VESC IDs are swapped.
  5. Tare (empty seat), then neutral, then range. Check that the dashboard shows the four "full" numbers.
  6. Sit up from a full lean: the wheels slow, stop, and resist a hand spin (held).
  7. DISARM at each knob setting: the wheels stop, then spin freely (rider seated).
  8. Unplug one VESC's CAN wire: "VESC silent", fast-blinking LED, both wheels braked until DISARM.
  9. Unplug one seat sensor: that direction goes dead, and nothing moves on its own.
  10. Remote: switch it off while driving → brakes. Switch it on with the stick held → nothing until you centre it. Stop button while in LEAN → brakes.
  11. Lift off the seat while driving → brakes and holds.
  12. Release a full turn: does it stop too sharply or too loosely? (Tune TURN_DECEL_DEG_S2.)
  13. Assist: spin a wheel by hand. You should get one fading boost per push and no runaway.
  14. Measure the track width at the floor and enter it.

This branch changes motor behaviour: get Charles's OK before uploading it.

Open questions for the team

  • Coasting. Should sitting up in LEAN glide, as in basketball, instead of brake? Should DISARM at speed let go instead of stopping at 0.38 g?
  • Spec B. Stops meet 0.5 s only on knob 1. Should we brake harder, limit the top speed, or rewrite the spec?
  • Turn rate. 160°/s vs. the spec's 200°/s. Is a 2.5 m/s² sideways limit right for a sports chair?
  • Reverse. Is half speed, plus "back to neutral before reversing", right for the rider?
  • Neutral not saved. Is pressing calibrate at every power-on OK?
  • Broken sensor wire. It can't be detected with two-wire sensors. Add a resistor so an open wire reads at the rail and trips the fault?
  • Faults in Assist. Only the boost drops and the chair isn't braked. OK?
  • Holding. Is creep on a slope acceptable, or do we want a real parking brake?
