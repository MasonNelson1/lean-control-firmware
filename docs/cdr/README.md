# Critical Design Review (text only)

`critical-design-review.txt` is the text of the team's Critical Design Review
(dated 5/8/2026, last edited 11/18), extracted from the Word document. The
original `.docx` is 117 MB, mostly full-size images, so it's too big for GitHub
and lives on the team Drive.

- The figures and tables-as-images are not in this text.
- The names of the rider and the sponsor are redacted.
- Parts of it are out of date, especially the control system, Bluetooth and the
  dashboard. Where they disagree, trust the firmware and `notes/`.
