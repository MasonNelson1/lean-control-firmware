E4: first look at all four sensors

Mason, config F5 B2 L5 R5, no plate bolts, wheels on, 2026-11-21 22:17–22:19. Load above zero force (raw − 372), hold averages.

What it shows
  • The plate rests on the two side sensors and the back. The front carries almost nothing until you lean forward.
  • The side sensors rise about as much as the front on a forward lean, so they can help detect forward (Charles's idea).
  • Left/right leans swing the side sensors 4–5× more than forward/back leans move front/back. That's why side gains of 0.01 still turned well.
  • In centre-of-pressure terms (bottom chart), passive and active neutral nearly overlap, and back-pivot lands with back-touch. The posture problem mostly disappears.
  • Right carries more than left even at neutral (rider or sensor asymmetry).
