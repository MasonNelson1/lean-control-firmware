**Findings from the real log**
- **The 1 Hz burst was losing lines before this work.** The once-a-second lines went out together, about 700 B at once. In today's tap the last of them, `DBG VESC`, arrived intact only 82–88% of the time; the 20 Hz rows were about 99.6% intact. You can see truncated lines like `vin= 425 409`. Each 1 Hz line now has its own 20 Hz tick, so the busiest tick is about 515 B.
- **Fault polling is 10 Hz, not the 5 Hz in the brief.** A VESC holds its fault code for 500 ms (`m_fault_stop_time_ms`). Alternating at 5 Hz asks each VESC only every 400 ms, so one skipped poll would miss a fault. At 10 Hz each VESC is asked every 200 ms.
- **One reply at a time.** VESC reply frames don't say which VESC sent them, so two replies at once mix their bytes. A new monitor sends one GET at a time, the existing probe queues behind it, and nothing is asked while `vescApplier.active()`.
- **Things already in the log:**
  - The left VESC sent no status frames at all around 16:00.
  - The right VESC showed 4.9 V input.
  - The `right-app` settings write failed with "no reply" at 16:04.
