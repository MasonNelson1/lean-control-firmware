The rider drives hands-free by leaning the torso: four force sensors under the seat (front/back/left/right), a Teensy 4.1, and two VESC motor controllers over CAN (left ID 2, right ID 26). 4. A recent "dynamic calibration" via the cal button (commit 7e90334) "worked and then didn't".

## 1. Findings, most severe first

**Safety**

1. **One broken sensor wire commands full speed.** `driveModes/LeanDriveMode.cpp:14` (since 7e90334, readings can go negative).
   - Calibrated at W=1000, the front sensor dropping to raw 0 or 400 gives speed −1.00 (full reverse); the back sensor gives +1.00; left or right gives a pivot. The seat check still passes.
   - Confidence: ran.
   - Fix: reject any sample where a sensor is outside a plausible band or jumps faster than a person can move, and go to zero output.

2. **The DISARM button is ignored when the seat is empty.** `ModeStateMachine.cpp:46-52`.
   - In REMOTE with nobody seated (the normal way to drive it remotely), pressing DISARM leaves the mode at REMOTE.
   - Confidence: ran.
   - Fix: exempt the disarm press from the unseated discard.

3. **Stale motor feedback makes the chair unstoppable in LEAN and REMOTE.** `main.cpp:68-75`; `vescFresh()` at `vesc_can.h:126` is never called.
   - The command is clamped to measured ±50 RPM. If status frames stop while a wheel reads 78 RPM, a zero target is sent as 28 RPM indefinitely.
   - Confidence: by reading.
   - Fix: if feedback is older than about 100 ms, drop the clamp and send zero current.

4. **No path meets "wheel speed under 5% within 0.50 s".** `main.cpp:433-437`, `driveModes/RemoteDriveMode.cpp:55-61`, the TODO at `main.cpp:27`.
   - Disarm and empty seat send zero current, so the chair coasts.
   - Remote timeout and return-to-neutral go through the smoother:

     | Speed knob | Starting speed | Time to under 5% |
     |---|---|---|
     | 1 | 1.23 m/s | 0.58 s |
     | 2 | 2.46 m/s | 0.76 s |
     | 3 | 3.69 m/s | 1.07 s |
     | 4 | 4.92 m/s | 1.38 s |

   - If the input drops mid-acceleration, the command keeps rising first (47 to 60 RPM).
   - The remote timeout is 100 ms plus up to one tick, so it is at or just over the 0.10 s limit.
   - Confidence: ran.
   - Fix: a dedicated stop path that bypasses the jerk limit and brakes actively. At 3.7 m/s² braking, 0.5 s is only reachable from about 1.8 m/s, so the spec or the limit has to move.

5. **No watchdog on the Teensy.** If the loop hangs, the last RPM setpoint stays live until the VESC's own timeout.
   - The reference config in `pcb/vesc-reference` shows 1000 ms and 0 A brake; I could not see the chair's actual VESC settings.
   - Fix: enable the Teensy watchdog and set the VESC timeout to 100 ms.

6. **Seat detection acts on a single 5 ms sample.** `driveModes/LeanDriveMode.h:40`, `ModeStateMachine.cpp:109-117`.
   - One low sample drops to DISARM (coast) and throws away calibration, so the rider must reach for the cal button while rolling.
   - Separately, if the calibrated average is below 458 counts the chair disarms itself right after calibrating. The one calibration in the logs (505/430/401/481) hits this, though I can't tell whether a rider was seated.
   - Confidence: ran.
   - Fix: require about 150 ms of continuous low load, and keep calibration through brief dropouts.

7. **Re-engaging while rolling brakes hard.** `main.cpp:373-388`, `main.cpp:70-75`.
   - After any smoother reset (cal press, seat glitch), the command restarts from 0 and is clamped to measured −50 RPM.
   - Confidence: by reading.
   - Fix: seed the smoother from measured RPM.

8. **ASSIST is positive feedback with no speed cutoff.** `driveModes/PushAssist.h:26`.
   - Boost rises with wheel speed and stays at full above the limit; on a slope it should run away.
   - Confidence: plausible, hardware-dependent.
   - Fix: taper boost to zero near the speed limit.

9. **REMOTE drive packets have no checksum or pairing.** `driveModes/RemoteDriveMode.cpp:13-30`.
   - Any 5-byte frame starting 0x01 is a drive command; config packets get a CRC-16.
   - Fix: add the same CRC.

**Control and calibration**

10. **Dynamic calibration can produce absurd gains.** `driveModes/LeanDriveMode.cpp:166-197`.
    - The only checks are peak ≥ 10% of W and difference > 0. A front peak of 300 with the rear at 290 gives a front gain of 100, after which 12 raw counts is full speed.
    - Peaks are single unfiltered samples.
    - The per-sensor formula is also wrong when the two sides differ: a clean calibration gave +1.06 forward and −0.83 back.
    - Confidence: ran.
    - Fix: clamp gains to about 0.5–4, and solve each axis as a pair.

11. **Holding the cal button over 300 ms kicks the chair to DISARM.** `driveModes/LeanDriveMode.cpp:139-146`, `main.cpp:391-395`.
    - Sitting still and holding 0.4 s is treated as a rejected dynamic calibration. Re-entering LEAN then needs calibration again.
    - Confidence: ran.
    - Fix: only disarm when gains actually changed.

12. **The dashboard's four sensor-gain fields do nothing at runtime.** `main.cpp:97-111`.
    - Gains are copied only at boot and on profile activation (`main.cpp:155`, `main.cpp:234`).
    - Dynamic-calibration gains live in RAM and vanish on power cycle or profile switch.
    - Confidence: by reading.
    - Fix: refresh them in `applyRuntimeSettings()` and persist the calibrated ones.

13. **Unequal gains turn total-load changes into drive commands.** `driveModes/LeanDriveMode.cpp:42-45,65`; defaults F=2, B=3 at `config/DriveSettings.cpp:68-75`.
    - All four sensors dropping 300 counts (rider pushing up on the rims) gives speed +0.156; rising gives reverse.
    - Confidence: ran.
    - Fix: one gain per axis, and divide by the live sum, `(F−B)/(F+B)`.

14. **Wheel RPM commands are truncated to whole RPM.** `vesc_can.h:78` takes `int32_t rpm`.
    - Anything under ±1 RPM becomes 0; at knob 1 that is 2.6% steps.
    - Confidence: by reading.
    - Fix: make the parameter `float`.

15. **Smaller items.**
    - Neutral calibration is a 40 ms snapshot taken on the button press, and it blocks the loop (up to 1 s at 500 samples), `driveModes/LeanDriveMode.cpp:104-113`.
    - The response-curve bounds allow exponents from about 0.0001 to 6900, which is a step function, `config/DriveSettings.cpp:44`.

## 2. The four complaints

**Turning overshoot.** Lean sets a turn *rate*, with nothing measuring heading, and three things stack on top:
- **Smoother lag.** A full-lean pivot at knob 2, released at 62°, ends at 95°. That is 33° of overshoot before any human reaction time (ran, assuming a 0.60 m track).
- **Per-wheel smoothing erases steering during acceleration.** Asking for L=0.60, R=0.30 from rest gives zero wheel difference for the first 0.25 s and a third of it at 0.4 s, `rpm_setpoint_pid.h:79`.
- **Turn gain triples with speed and scales with the speed knob.** The same lean gives a wheel difference of 0.23 at rest and 0.57 at 60% speed; full-lean pivot is about 80°/s on knob 1 and 318°/s on knob 4 (`config/DriveSettings.cpp:37-38`).

The −0.3 reverse flip (`driveModes/LeanDriveMode.cpp:81`) swaps the wheel difference in one tick (L −0.07/R −0.51 becomes L −0.53/R −0.08). I'm not calling it a bug, but it needs a blend or hysteresis around the tuned value.

What I'd change: smooth speed and turn as two axes instead of per wheel, make turn gain flat or falling with speed, and express the turn limit in deg/s independent of the speed knob.

**Tuning and "nothing then everything".** The default curve is mild (exponent 1.54), so it is not the main cause.
- **Check first, on the chair:** the VESC's minimum speed-PID ERPM. Its default is 900 ERPM as I recall, about 15.5 wheel RPM, below which the motor is released. If that's unchanged, a pivot at knob 2 does nothing until about 70% lean. I could not verify this from the repo.
- **Sensitivity doubled in 7e90334.** Both sensors of a pair now count, so a front/back shift of 20% of W is already full speed; older tuning no longer applies.
- **Dead knobs.** The gain fields don't apply (finding 12), and dashboard Save writes the active config, which the profile on the knob overwrites at boot (`main.cpp:228-246`).

I'd cut the knobs to three: forward sensitivity, turn sensitivity, max turn rate.

**Different after reseating.** Neutral is a 40 ms snapshot taken while the rider reaches for the button. Capturing 30 counts off-centre already produces a −0.054 creep at true neutral (ran). The divisor W is the raw average including the unloaded baseline, so it shifts with posture too. Average neutral over about 1 s with a stillness check, and normalise by live load.

**"Worked and then didn't".** Findings 10–12: gains are lost on power cycle or profile switch, a slightly long cal press disarms the chair, and single-sample peaks make each run land on different gains.
