S1: how the lean signal grows through a slow lean

Mason, config F5 B2 L5 R5, 2026-11-21 22:40–22:41. One slow 5-count lean per direction, from active neutral to full (back = back-touch). Signal = how far the centre of pressure has moved from neutral, computed from the 20 Hz raw sensors.

What it shows
  • No dead zone at the start in any direction. With centre-of-pressure math the sensors respond from the first moment of the lean. The "lean, lean, nothing, then everything" feel comes from the current firmware math, not the sensors.
  • Back is the weak axis: it rises, then goes flat from about 20% to 60% of the lean before climbing again, and it has the smallest total range.
  • Left/right give the most range and the steadiest growth. Forward is in between.
  • Resolution (bottom chart): how many steps between neutral and full lean the chair could tell apart, given sensor noise. Back ≈ 6 steps vs ≈ 22 for left/right.
